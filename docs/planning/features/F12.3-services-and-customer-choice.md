### US-260 Your kirana, marketing and khata (ADR 0107 §15)

As the player running shops, I want my own kirana shop, a marketing budget and credit for regular customers (khata), so that I run a shop the way village shops really run.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S26
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028 (ADR 0107), within the scope below
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13, §15):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A kirana (grocery) of your own on a pitch or acre, competing with the village grocer by spec 017's rules; a marketing budget lifting a shop's attraction while spent; khata: regular households buy on credit and pay at the season's close, a receivable on the shop's books with some never paid (spec 017's Phase 3 list).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Loyalty schemes and chains (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The marketing effect, khata's limits and losses (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
