### US-142 Zoning

As the player, I want to zone land and watch builders fill it, so that the town grows itself.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S22
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** From the Township you zone acres residential (kutcha and pucca houses, 2 an acre), commercial (shops run by townspeople under the rivals' rules, spec 017) or agricultural (stays farmland). On land you own, a zoned acre is offered to builders at its land value and leaves your land when sold; on the ring's unowned land builders buy from its owner. A builder builds when households want homes (migrants, growing families) and the acre has road access and water; each build pays a permit fee to the treasury and needs masons and bricks (US-145, US-243).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Industrial and mixed-use zones; more than 2 homes an acre (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The builders' demand rule and the permit fee (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-143 Land value

As the player, I want a land value overlay, so that I see what makes places valuable.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S22
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A value for every acre from road access, the services within reach (school, clinic, square, water), jobs within reach and the town's size, less crime. It sets rent's location from the Township (US-240), property tax (US-140), where builders build, and the price of land you buy or sell, which rises as the town grows (ADR 0028's payback re-measured by the bots).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Noise and pollution (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The weights of each part (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
