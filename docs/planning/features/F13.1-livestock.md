## Summary

From the Hamlet: a 4-animal cattle shed in the yard, Murrah buffalo and crossbred cows from the village fair, milk to the dairy co-op every 6:00 paid by fat, feeding every morning (the commons, berseem and sorghum cut daily, bought green fodder, straw from the harvest, bought cattle feed), the lactation cycle and calves, health with vaccination and the vet, dung to compost, the hand's animals order and the dairy loan. Goats and poultry come later; no pigs.
