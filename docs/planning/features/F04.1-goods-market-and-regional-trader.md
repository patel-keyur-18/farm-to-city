### US-252 A mandi trip with several crops (owner request, 2026-11-13, ADR 0107 §14)

As the player, I want to load several harvested crops on one mandi trip, filling the trolley with what pays best, so that I don't lose profit to the trader on any of them.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S20
- **Source:** owner request (2026-11-13); spec 004 §3.7 amended in this story; the sim already takes several loads (`GoToMandi`)
- **Acceptance:**
  - The mandi sheet lists every crop in your store with what the mandi pays a kg after commission beside the trader's price, best gain first (`MandiViewTests.SeveralCrops`).
  - Each crop's load is dragged on a slider or typed (the savings sheet's control, in kg, steps of 50 kg), from 0 to the lesser of what's free in the store and the trolley's room left after the other crops (`MandiViewTests.RoomLeft`).
  - *Fill with what pays best* loads crops in order of the mandi's gain over the trader a kg until the trolley is full, leaving out any crop the trader pays as much for; you can change any load after (`MandiViewTests.FillBest`).
  - The sum shows each crop's line, the commission, the hire, what you get and what the trader would pay for the same loads; *Go* sends one trip with every load (`GoToMandi`, unchanged).
  - Editor: loading two or more crops, filling, changing a load and going, by scripted touch; the layout previewed to the owner before code.

**Tasks**

- [x] US-252.T1 The sheet previewed as an interactive artifact in the game's own look; approved 2026-11-13
- [x] US-252.T2 View: the mandi sheet's rows with `AmountVm` in kg (room left across crops, `Span` and `Then`), *Fill with what pays best*, the sum; the old per-crop load sheet removed; `mandi.loadStepKg` in `market.json`
- [x] US-252.T3 Presentation: the kg slider rows (the presenter's amount row, without a button per row; the sheet redrawn in place when a slider is let go or a kg typed, its scroll kept); Editor check by scripted touch (`docs/art/s20-252-slider.png`, `s20-252-sum.png`)
- [x] US-252.T4 Tests (`MandiViewTests`); spec 004 §3.6 *As built*, AC-25
