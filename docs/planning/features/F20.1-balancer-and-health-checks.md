### US-098 Phase 1 bots and checks

As the developer (Claude), I want cautious, aggressive-borrower and random bots with Phase 1 health checks, so that Phase 1 is proven fair before testers play.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S8
- **Acceptance:** No money loops, never stuck, recoverable bankruptcy, exact ledger, bounded prices: all pass (ADR 0045 §4; thresholds measured, then set realistically)

**Tasks**

- [x] US-098.T1 Bots (`tools/Tools.Balancer/Bots.cs`: `CautiousSaverBot`, `RandomBot`, `RecoveringBot`; `FarmerBot.MayBorrow` and `ReadyToHire`): the cautious saver, random, and a recovering defaulter; the leverage bot as debt-fuelled expansion (`GateTests.Phase1_StressBots`)
- [x] US-098.T2 Phase 1 checks in CI (`Gate.NeverStuck`, `Recovery`, `ExactLedger`, `RailShare`; measured on 100 seeds: never stuck 100% by season 2, recovery 84 of 84 profitable in 1 season and the Hamlet again in a median of 8.0 days (max 9.6), ledger pass, rail 0.000%; median farm profit 26,048, in spec 003 §9's band, so no tuning): `GateTests.NeverStuck` (credit refused), `GateTests.RecoverableBankruptcy`, `GateTests.ExactLedger`, `GateTests.BoundedPrices`; the per-bot report with farm profit against spec 003 §9

### US-099 Archetype bots

As the developer (Claude), I want agri-hub, merchant and education bots, so that the 20% balance rule can be enforced (ADR 0016).

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S20
- **Acceptance:** ADR 0016 on Phase 2's systems now, extended with each Phase 3 spec: the agri-hub, merchant and education bots finish within 20% of each other on median net worth and happiness, and no stress bot beats the best archetype by more than 20%, over 200 seeds to game year 8 (ADR 0107 §11; the full 1,000 seeds at year 12 at the Phase 4 gate). `ArchetypeTests.Archetypes_WithinTwentyPercent` (category Balance); a miss goes to the owner with its numbers.

**Tasks**

- [x] US-099.T1 `AgriHubBot` (land, irrigation, orchards, the godown), `MerchantBot` (the mill, bakery, dairy and shops), `EducationBot` (the school and adult classes early), each from the competent bot (`tools/Tools.Balancer/Archetypes.cs`)
- [x] US-099.T2 `ArchetypeTests.Archetypes_WithinTwentyPercent`: medians and the 20% rule, and the stress bots against the best archetype; 24 seeds by default, 200 with `FTC_SEEDS=200` (about 10 minutes; the owner's numbers in ADR 0112)
- [x] US-099.T3 The numbers to the owner (ADR 0112): the rule passes once every archetype plants the orchard (it failed at 1.30 with the orchard left to the agri-hub)

### US-101 Device performance per phase

As the owner, I want a frame-time and catch-up check on the iPhone 13 at the end of every phase, so that performance never degrades unnoticed (ADR 0026).

- **Points:** 3 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S20
- **Acceptance:** ADR 0094's limits on the iPhone 13 (main thread p95 ≤ 8.3 ms, GPU p95 ≤ 11 ms, about 60 fps, under 1% over 25 ms) and catch-up under 2 s, measured at Phase 3's first Development build (closing Phase 2's deferred p95s and the phone's golden replays, ADR 0106) and again with the map's ring (ADR 0107 §12). In the Editor, every visible story reads the rendering statistics (draw calls, batches, sprites) at the most crowded view and flags growth over 10% against the last reading.

**Tasks**

- [x] US-101.T1 An Editor helper (`Game.Editor.RenderStats`) that frames the most crowded view (the whole map; also the village and the farm) and returns the rendering statistics, flagging growth over 10% against the last reading in `docs/planning/render-stats.json`; the first reading recorded in STATUS (Environment notes); the rule added to `CLAUDE.md`
- [ ] US-101.T2 Sprint 20's Development build: both p95s and golden replays 001, 003, 004, 005 and the 72 h catch-up on the phone (the owner), written down in the checklist

### US-248 Phase 3 exploit checks (ADR 0107 §11)

As the owner, I want a stress bot or a test for every Phase 3 money path, so that the gate's "the balancer finds no exploits" means something measurable.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S26
- **Source:** ADR 0107 §11, ADR 0016
- **Acceptance:** zero-time loops over every new command pair; no route from the treasury to the founder's wallet; an *insure everything* bot no better on the median than an uninsured one; no riskless forward contract; a found-and-sell bot not over 20% above the competent bot; a town with every tax at 0 loses services and happiness; a landlord raising every rent to the cap no better than a fair one; late payment of your own invoices makes no money; never stuck, an exact ledger, bounded prices kept. 200 seeds to game year 8. Each its own named test in `GateTests` (category Balance).

- [ ] Broken down into tasks when specs 021–028 are written (rolling wave, ADR 0019).
