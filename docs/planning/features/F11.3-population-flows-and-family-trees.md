## Summary

Families move into empty houses and leave when unhappy or behind on rent; a hand can bring his family (his house part of his pay); couples have children, children marry, elders pass on gently; the family tree stays bounded; every bot reaches the Village's households.

### US-109 Births, marriages and deaths

As the player, I want families to grow and elders to pass on, so that the town feels alive.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S14
- **Acceptance:** spec 014 AC-6; §3.9 tone; ADR 0070 (marriage is patrilocal)

**Tasks**

- [x] US-109.T1 Births (0.10 × room × happiness, at most 3), marriages (22–30, 0.25; a son's bride into a free house or with his parents, a daughter marries away: ADR 0070), deaths of elders only (1.5% at 60 doubling every 8 years, × 1.5 in poor health, capped 0.5) on the seeded streams
- [x] US-109.T2 Mourning (−10 for a season); a widowed elder stays with family; gentle wording, information only, no push; the away card's born, married and passed-away lines (moved from US-210.T5)
- [x] US-109.T3 Tests: `PopulationTests.Flows`, `PopulationTests.NoYoungDeaths`

### US-110 Migration: families in and out, a hand's family, every path

As the player, I want people to move in for homes and work and out when unhappy, so that my choices change who lives here.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S14
- **Acceptance:** spec 014 AC-3, AC-7, AC-11, AC-14

**Tasks**

- [ ] US-110.T1 A family every 18 ± 6 h into an empty house (2–5 people, drawn as applicants), live or away; rent from the next close
- [ ] US-110.T2 A free house offered first to a hand living alone (`FamilyRequest`, `AnswerFamilyRequest`; strangers after Not now or 18 h away); the hand's family card on screen (Yes / Not now, "the wage is 6,000 less from the next renewal"; moved from US-210.T4) and the moved-in away line and notification; housing in kind at his next renewal (a loyal hand's kept wage too); full wage after they move out; the rent and happiness *Why?* rows for moving out
- [ ] US-110.T3 Moving out (below 35 for 2 closes, or 2 seasons behind; 50% a close); a hand's household leaving ends his contract; a dismissed hand's family stays as tenants; an elder alone behind on rent goes to family
- [ ] US-110.T4 Tests: `MigrationTests2.FamilyMovesIn`, `MovingOut`, `HandFamily_HousingInKind`, `EveryPath`
