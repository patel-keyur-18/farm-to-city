### US-221 Your season, earned and spent (owner request, 2026-10-28; ADR 0083)

As the player, I want Money and loans to open on exactly what I earned and what I spent this season, line by line, and what's already due next season, so that I see my profit and where every coin went without land values in the way.

- **Points:** 7 (ADR 0083: 5 + 2 for the sales record and save schema 12) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S15 (owner, 2026-10-29); spec 019 amended (§3.9) and re-approved first
- **Acceptance:** spec 019 AC-11–AC-16

**Overlap (pushback, 2026-10-28):** spec 019 (approved, ADR 0078) already redesigns the season report as three statements and Money and loans in the Store/People layout, in Sprint 18 (US-137 statements, US-135 the hub). Building this separately would build that screen twice. Recommended: amend spec 019 so its first screen is this breakdown (balance sheet and land values on a second tab, kept but not in the way), and bring the work forward if wanted.

**What the breakdown shows**

- **Earned:** each crop sold, milk, orders, rent from families, your businesses' drawings, day labour, interest on the deposit.
- **Spent:** seed, fertiliser, sprays; your hand's wage (each hand by name); day labourers; feed and the vet; living costs; rent paid; loan payments split into interest (a cost) and principal (paying back what you borrowed, not a cost: shown, with a *Why?*, because profit and cash are different things, the game's core lesson).
- **Profit this season** and **cash left**, each with a *Why?*.
- **Next season, already due:** only what's committed (hands' wages due at the close, loan instalments, rent), never guesses.
