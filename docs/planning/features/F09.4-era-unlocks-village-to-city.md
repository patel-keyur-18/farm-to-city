### US-102 Village unlock

As the player, I want my hamlet to become a village when it qualifies, so that each era feels earned.

- **Points:** 5 (was 2, ADR 0095) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S17
- **Acceptance:** spec 018 AC-8, AC-9 (ADR 0077, 0095)

**Tasks**

- [x] US-102.T1 `eras.json` Village gate (5 households, one business with 2 profitable seasons, the school open; the `manual` flag gone), checked at each close, when the school opens and when a household forms or moves in (ADR 0095 §9); profitable seasons counted on each business (schema 15; an existing business's from its ledger at the migration); `EraUnlocked(village)`; play-log milestone
- [x] US-102.T2 The `village` chapter on `EraUnlocked(village)` (the era-id filter itself was built in Sprint 15, `GuideViews.Name`), its letter's text previewed to the owner first; "Becoming a Village" progress (`ProgressSheet` made general) with each row's next step; the Hamlet celebration names the goals; the celebration lists what opens, read from content with `unlockEra: "village"`
- [x] US-102.T3 `VillageBot` (the competent bot plus a mill at the Hamlet and the school on a co-op loan once room allows) and a saver variant (no loans); tests `EraTests.Village`, `EraTests.Village_OnHousehold`, `GateTests.Village` (competent 14–21 days, saver 28–42); a miss goes to the owner with its numbers. As built: ADR 0097 (the owner accepted the measured pace, 2026-11-10: homes first 28–40 days with loans and 50–64 saving, every order reachable); also `EraTests.Village` (view)
