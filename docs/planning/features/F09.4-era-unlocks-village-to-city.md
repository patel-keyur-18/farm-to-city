### US-103 Township unlock

As the player, I want my village to become a township when it qualifies, so that each era feels earned.

The gate (ADR 0107 §5): 20 of your own households, the clinic open with its doctor, the paved link road, 3 open businesses each with a profitable season, literacy at 60% or more; the Township at 9–11 weeks using credit, about 15 saving. At the unlock the old village joins the town as a cohort and the map grows its ring (§2).

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S22
- **Acceptance:** From its spec (ADR 0107), within the scope below
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** ADR 0107 §5's gate, checked at each close and when a goal changes; the *Becoming a Township* progress sheet with each goal's next step; the Township letter and celebration listing what opens (from content); at the unlock the old village joins the town as a cohort (about 30 households and 150 people) and the map's ring arrives (US-253).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** The Town and City unlocks (US-104, Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The cohort's numbers (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
