### US-146 Roads and highway

As the player, I want to build roads and a highway link, so that trade gets cheaper.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S22
- **Acceptance:** From spec 023/025
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** From the Village with 10 households you petition at the council hall; the district builds a paved link road for a community share (about 50,000, a gift on card B) in about 4 days. The district road on the map's south edge is drawn paved; the road tier becomes *road* (1.00 a kg); every trader price's *Why?* names the road. It is one of the Township's goals.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** The highway tier (Phase 4's bonds); lanes inside the ring (US-253).
  - **The spec sets:** The share, the days (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-147 Hill parcels and apples

- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**

  - **Not in Phase 3:** Other hill crops.
  - **The spec sets:** The apple orchard's numbers (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-253 The map's ring (ADR 0107 §2)

As the player, I want my town's land to grow when it becomes a Township, so that there is room to zone, build and farm the hills.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025 (ADR 0107), within the scope below
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** At the Township unlock the map grows to about 24 × 18 acres (one save migration shifting tile ids); the ring holds land for zoning, hill parcels, more of the old village drawn and the highway's side. The council builds gravel lanes to open zones (the treasury, card B). The layout is previewed to the owner first and never cramped (ADR 0064).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Paved roads and avenues inside the town, congestion (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The exact size and layout, the lanes' cost (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
