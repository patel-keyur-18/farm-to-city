## Summary

The trader comes as a 4 t truck from 6:00 to midnight with a price set each morning; the stall closes at night; a city supermarket posts two crop orders and a small greens order every 6:00, for produce harvested that day, paid the next morning; rice and potato grow in 15 hours.
