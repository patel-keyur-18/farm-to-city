### US-159 Citizen stories

As the player, I want short stories about notable citizens, so that I care about the people of my town.

- **Points:** 5 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S26
- **Acceptance:** From spec 029
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Arcs from the simulation, each a short vignette as its step happens: a hand who saves and opens a cart or stall (a newcomer business run by him), the first literate child of a family, an elder's long life, a migrant family that stays.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** The scholarship child who returns as a doctor (needs a college, the Town).
  - **The spec sets:** The arcs' steps (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
