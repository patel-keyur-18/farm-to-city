## Summary

Redesign everything drawn on the map in Blender, one category at a time, in the realistic style the owner picked from three samples (ADR 0180), at about 60 fps with no dropped frames and no heating. The UI follows in spec 033.

### US-271 The art inventory and three samples

As the owner, I want a list of everything drawn and three samples in two styles, so that I pick the direction before the redesign.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S29
- **Tasks:**
  - [x] US-271.T1 The inventory: every sprite by category and what is drawn in code ([docs/art/inventory.md](../../art/inventory.md), 605 sprites)
  - [x] US-271.T2 Three samples (a family house, a wheat field, a farm hand and his wife) rendered today, rich stylised and realistic, shown as Preview 27; the owner picked realistic, Option B (`tools/blender/samples.py`, `docs/art/s29-samples/`)
  - [x] US-271.T3 Spec 032 written from the pick (the style, the lighting, the sizes, the shadows, the camera, the frame-time guard) and approved (2026-11-27, ADR 0180)
- **Acceptance:** Every drawn thing listed (sprites, and what is drawn in code); a family house, a wheat field and a person rendered realistic and rich stylised, shown as a preview; the owner's pick written into spec 032.

### US-273 to US-282 The redesign, one category a story

As the player, I want the valley drawn realistic with far more detail, so that it feels alive and real while I play.

- **Points:** 53 in all (and US-283's 2 above: 55, spec 032 §12) · **Priority:** Should (effects Could) · **Sprints:** S30–S33
- **Acceptance (each):** every sprite of its category re-rendered realistic at 8× (today's world size, spec 032 §3.2), a before/after preview approved before the renders go in; the Editor tour by scripted touch at the most crowded view with a `RenderStats` reading (growth over 10% reported); no new shaders or particles; every capture looked at; at the new default zoom (3 acres across).
- **Tasks:** spec 032 §12 (the plan) and each story's plan check.
