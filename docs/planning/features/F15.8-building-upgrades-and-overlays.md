### US-152 Building upgrades

As the player, I want homes to upgrade as the town can support them, so that the skyline grows from real prosperity.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A kutcha house becomes pucca when land value, the household's income and water allow: yours for the difference in cost (card B), a builder's by itself; a hand's hut can become a kutcha house; the tenant's rent moves at the lease's renewal (US-240).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Villas, row houses and apartments (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The thresholds (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-153 Overlays

As the player, I want map overlays, so that I can see hidden information.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Map overlays, one at a time from a button on the map: fertility, land value, coverage (school, clinic, watchman, water), happiness, crime. A tap on an acre shows its value with a *Why?*. The coverage overlay also shows the generator's smoke.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Pollution beyond the generator's smoke, and traffic (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The colour scales (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
