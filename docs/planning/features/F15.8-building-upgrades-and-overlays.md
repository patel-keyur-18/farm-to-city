### US-152 Building upgrades

As the player, I want homes to upgrade as the town can support them, so that the skyline grows from real prosperity.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A kutcha house becomes pucca when land value, the household's income and water allow: yours for the difference in cost (card B), a builder's by itself; a hand's hut can become a kutcha house; the tenant's rent moves at the lease's renewal (US-240).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Villas, row houses and apartments (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The thresholds (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

**Tasks**

- [x] **T1** Content: `township.json` `upgrade` (land value 1.15, pucca rent 7,000) and the `pucca_upgrade` construction kind; loader and defs.
- [x] **T2** State: `Building.Pucca` (schema 21, hashed once used); the pucca market rent in `LeaseRules`.
- [x] **T3** `UpgradeHouse`, `UpgradeRules` (the checks, the order, the finish, the foreclosure's cancel), events `HouseUpgradeStarted` and `HouseUpgraded`.
- [x] **T4** Builders' upgrade: one home a close, bricks after yours.
- [x] **T5** View: the acre's rows and button, the card, the work under way, the family sheet's rent steps, the Builders count, *Why?* for every number, the away row.
- [x] **T6** Art: `pucca_house_*` and the scaffolded house in Blender; the map draws them.
- [x] **T7** Tests: `UpgradeTests` (8), `UpgradeViewTests` (6), `LandValueViewTests.EveryPartHasWords`; fixtures regenerated; Editor tour (`docs/art/s23-152-*.png`).
- [x] **T8** ADR 0131; spec 024 §3.7 as built. The hut → kutcha upgrade is cut (ADR 0131 §5).

### US-153 Overlays

As the player, I want map overlays, so that I can see hidden information.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 024
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Map overlays, one at a time from a button on the map: fertility, land value, coverage (school, clinic, watchman, water), happiness, crime. A tap on an acre shows its value with a *Why?*. The coverage overlay also shows the generator's smoke.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Pollution beyond the generator's smoke, and traffic (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The colour scales (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

**Tasks**

- [x] **T1** View: `OverlayView` (kinds, per-acre steps, the key's labels, the smoke footprint, the tap card), the coverage and happiness *Why?* sources, `HudVm.OverlaysAvailable`.
- [x] **T2** Strings: `overlay.*` and `why.coverage.*`, `why.acreHappiness.*`.
- [x] **T3** Map: the tint and the smoke hatch as two meshes (`MapPresenter.Overlays.cs`), the hatch texture, the selection ring above the tint.
- [x] **T4** UI: the Overlays button (green, "Close"), the chooser, the key and the tap card (`OverlayPresenter`); `GameRoot.Overlays.cs` (one at a time, a tap opens the card, a sheet ends the overlay).
- [x] **T5** Tests: `OverlayViewTests` (11); Editor tour by scripted touch (`docs/art/s23-153-*.png`) and render statistics.
- [x] **T6** ADR 0133; spec 024 §3.8 as built.
