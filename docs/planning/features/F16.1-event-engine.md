### US-154 Events

As the player, I want events with 2–3 choices, so that my preparation is tested.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 027
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Event families: weather (drought, flood, heatwave, hailstorm, on top of the weather spells), economic (a price boom or slump, a fuel spike), business (a supplier strike, a health inspection, a viral rush, a breakdown), workforce (a skilled migrant, poaching), social (a wedding, a dispute, a new family), opportunity (a regional grant, a land auction, an investor's offer, US-247), disaster (fire, epidemic). Each offers 2–3 choices with costs shown and long-term effects hinted; about one minor a real day and one major a game year; weighted by the town's state; disasters signalled days ahead and never fired while you're away; a minor event while you're away takes its default (the safest, named) and is reported. A fire's damage is softer within the water tower's reach and covered by property insurance (no fire station until the Town).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** River floods (need an embankment), recessions (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each event's weights, choices and numbers (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
