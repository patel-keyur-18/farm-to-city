### US-256 Your tractor for neighbours (custom hiring) (ADR 0107 §15)

As the player with a tractor, I want my hand to plough neighbours' fields for a fee, so that an idle machine earns.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Could · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025 (ADR 0107), within the scope below
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13, §15):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A hand drives your tractor on neighbours' fields on idle days for a fee an acre (spec 013's "custom hiring as a business: Phase 3"); the diesel and wear are yours; it shows on the machine's sheet and in your season.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** A tractor-hire business with its own books (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The fee, the days, the wear (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-257 A tube well an acre and solar pumps (ADR 0107 §15)

As the player, I want a tube well on a single acre and a solar pump, so that irrigation has a clean, cheaper-to-run step.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Could · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025 (ADR 0107), within the scope below
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13, §15):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A tube well watering just its own acre (spec 013's "a tube well per acre: Phase 3"); a solar pump instead of grid or diesel power for a pump or borewell, dearer to buy, free to run (half subsidised as PM-KUSUM does).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Solar power for the town (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Costs, subsidy, output (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
