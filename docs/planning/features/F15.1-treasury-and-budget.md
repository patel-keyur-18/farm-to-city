### US-138 Treasury

As the player, I want to run a separate town treasury, so that I learn public versus personal finance.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S21
- **Acceptance:** From spec 021
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A second wallet from the Township unlock with its own ledger accounts and three statements in spec 019's style. Income: the district's start-up grant, a per-resident grant each season with conditions (the clinic staffed), the square's pitch rents, taxes (US-140), building-permit fees (US-142). Spending by department: Education (the school's upkeep), Health (the clinic's upkeep and the doctor's allowance), Roads (the link road's and lanes' upkeep), the council's office (a clerk's wage), and Safety and Utilities once built. Buildings the town builds are its assets and depreciate. Your gifts come in as Gifts to the village; nothing ever goes from it to you or your businesses (no payment, subsidy, grant or contract). It never goes below zero: at the close departments are paid in budget order, an unpaid service decays, a day's warning comes first. A *Town treasury* sheet in the Menu; spending on confirm cards; a *Why?* on every number.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Bonds, public debt and a credit rating (F18.4); elections and council mood (F18.2); conflicts of interest and scandal (F18.4).
  - **The spec sets:** The grants' amounts and conditions, the clerk's wage, the account numbers (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-139 Budget cycle

As the player, I want to set and revise a budget, so that I learn budgeting.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S21
- **Acceptance:** From spec 021
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** An annual budget by department, set at the Spring close (the first at the unlock, for the rest of that year) and revisable once at the Monsoon close. A proposal is filled in for you (last year's actuals, else each department's upkeep), accepted with one tap. Each close reports actual against budget per department with the variance explained. A department past its budget is still paid while cash lasts, the overspend shown; one under its needs runs its service at a lower quality (fewer clinic hours, a road wearing) with the effect named.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Revisions needing a council vote (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The quality curve under-funding follows (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
