### US-144 Utilities

As the player, I want to build utilities, so that my town can grow safely.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** The utilities' Phase 3 steps, each built by the treasury on a confirm card with its upkeep in the budget, a reach and a capacity: water (wells, then a water tower), power (none, then a diesel generator, then a grid connection), sanitation (pits, then drains), waste (none, then collection). Without water, new homes stop and sickness rises; without drains, disease and lower land value; without collection, unhappiness; power gives households' leisure and businesses' evening hours. The diesel generator's smoke lowers health and land value within its reach, so the grid connection is worth its cost (the GDD's pollution from diesel power).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** A treatment plant, a sewage plant, recycling, solar or wind, a power plant (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each step's cost, reach and capacity (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-145 Construction

As the player, I want buildings to need materials, builders and time, so that a brick shortage really slows the town.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** From the Township every new building (yours, the treasury's and builders') needs bricks, cement and timber, masons from the hiring board (a mason trade) and build time. Materials come from the trader at the regional price, limited a day, or from your kiln (US-243). A shortage holds a build and says why. Buildings before the Township keep their Phase 2 rule (time only).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Steel; a construction firm (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each building's materials and hours (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
