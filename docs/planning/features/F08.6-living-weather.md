### US-222 The weather on the map

As the player, I want to see the weather on my farm, so that the valley feels alive and I notice rain and storms without reading the time strip.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** after Sprint 15 (owner's choice)
- **Acceptance:** to be written with the owner; a look previewed first (as the art is)

**Tasks**

- [ ] US-222.T1 Each of the 7 weather types drawn over the whole map from the current run: sun (bright, warm light, crisp shadows), cloudy (softer light, drifting cloud shadows), rain (falling streaks, darker ground, puddle glints), heavy rain (denser, greyer, splashes), storm (dark sky, gusting trees and crops, an occasional soft lightning glow), heatwave (warm haze, shimmer), frost (pale rime on fields at dawn, melting by mid-morning); changes ease in over a few seconds when a run changes; night darkens them all
- [ ] US-222.T2 Look previewed to the owner before it's final; fields, crops and people stay readable through it
- [ ] US-222.T3 Reduced motion (Settings, ADR 0043): rain and gusts lighter and still; no flashing at all (photosensitivity); frame time measured in the Editor and on the iPhone 13 against the half-frame budget (ADR 0026, 0035)
- [ ] US-222.T4 Editor tour by scripted touch through each weather (developer panel); a device check

**Notes (pushbacks, 2026-10-28)**

- **Sound:** rain on the tin roofs and thunder fit, but ADR 0044 keeps sounds to town events; adding ambient sound is the owner's call when this is taken up.
- **Lightning** as a soft glow, never a strobe (accessibility).
- One weather for the whole valley, as the sim has it (no rain on one field and sun on the next).
