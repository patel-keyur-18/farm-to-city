### US-222 The weather on the map

As the player, I want to see the weather on my farm, so that the valley feels alive and I notice rain and storms without reading the time strip.

- **Points:** 6 (ADR 0083: 5 + 1 for sound) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S15 (owner, 2026-10-29)
- **Acceptance:** spec 008 AC-19–AC-21; the look previewed to and approved by the owner before code

**Tasks**

- [ ] US-222.T1 The look previewed to the owner (an interactive page over real captures, day and night, the 7 weathers and reduced motion; stills `docs/art/s15-222-weather-*.png`) and approved; its numbers into `content/ui.json` (`weatherLook`)
- [ ] US-222.T2 Each of the 7 weathers drawn over the whole map for the whole spell: sun (warm light, crisp shadows), cloudy (softer light, drifting cloud shadows), rain (falling streaks, darker ground), heavy rain (denser, greyer, splashes), storm (dark sky, gusting trees and crops, a soft lightning glow), heatwave (warm haze, shimmer), frost (pale rime, cold light; the whole spell, a spell being a world day, ADR 0083 §16); eased over a few seconds at a change; night darkens them all; fields, crops and people readable
- [ ] US-222.T3 No flashing for anyone (glow ≥ 0.3 s rise, ≤ once in 15–25 s, never > 3 a second); reduced motion still (no moving layer, no glow); tests `WeatherLookTests.FollowsSpell`, `ReducedMotion_Still`, `Lightning_NoFlash`
- [ ] US-222.T4 Sound: a CC0 rain loop and distant thunder behind the Sound switch, fading with the weather, logged in `ASSETS_LICENSES.md` (amends ADR 0044 §2)
- [ ] US-222.T5 The developer panel's look-only **Weather look ▸**; frame time in every weather in the Editor; checklist rows for the iPhone 13 (storm at night, heavy rain, whole map); the Editor tour by scripted touch through each weather

**Notes (pushbacks, 2026-10-28)**

- **Sound:** rain on the tin roofs and thunder fit, but ADR 0044 keeps sounds to town events; adding ambient sound is the owner's call when this is taken up. **Settled (owner, 2026-10-29, ADR 0083 §14):** a rain loop and distant thunder behind the Sound switch.
- **Lightning** as a soft glow, never a strobe (accessibility).
- One weather for the whole valley, as the sim has it (no rain on one field and sun on the next).
