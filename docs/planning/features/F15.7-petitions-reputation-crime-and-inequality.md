### US-150 Petitions

As the player, I want citizens to raise petitions, so that I hear what my town needs.

- **Points:** 3 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 026
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Households raise petitions from their unmet needs (a service out of reach, a rent too high, no work), one at a time per topic. You answer: do it, promise it by a date, or decline. Answered ones raise approval, ignored or broken ones lower it; approval (0–100) is shown and in Phase 3 moves migration only.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Protests and elections (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Which needs raise which petitions (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-151 Reputation and fairness

As the player, I want town reputation, crime and inequality to respond to my choices, so that fairness has visible effects.

- **Points:** 5 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 026
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Five reputation scores (livability, business climate, education, health, environment), each from named parts, drawing migrants and adding to grants. Crime from unemployment and inequality, lowered by the watchman and, more cheaply, by jobs and fair wages. The inequality indicator (the richest tenth's income over the poorest tenth's), high inequality lowering happiness and pushing families out.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Scandal from conflicts of interest (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each score's parts and weights (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
