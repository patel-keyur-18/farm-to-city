### US-123 Invoices

As the player, I want sales between businesses on payment terms, so that I learn a profitable business can still run out of cash.

- **Points:** 5 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S25
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** From the Township every sale between businesses (yours to yours, yours to the village grocer and tea stall, theirs to yours) is invoiced on half-season or full-season terms (the seller chooses per buyer), a receivable and a payable on each business's own books, paid when due from the buyer's cash or overdraft; a late payer's invoices age and its suppliers may stop; its cash flow shows the difference from profit. Households still pay cash.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Invoices with the trader and the supermarket.
  - **The spec sets:** Ageing, the stop rule (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
