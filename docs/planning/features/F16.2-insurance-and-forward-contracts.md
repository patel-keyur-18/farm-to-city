### US-155 Insurance

As the player, I want to insure against risks, so that I learn certain small costs versus uncertain large ones.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 027
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Crop insurance per acre a season (pays when weather or a pest outbreak cuts the yield below a threshold, like PMFBY), property insurance (buildings against fire) and business-interruption insurance (a business's lost profit after a fire or a strike). Premiums are priced above the expected payout; bought on card B at the close for a season.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Life and health insurance.
  - **The spec sets:** The thresholds, premiums and loadings (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-156 Forward contracts

As the player, I want fixed-price contracts with the trader, so that I learn hedging.

- **Points:** 3 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 027
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** The trader offers a fixed price for a quantity of a crop next season; you accept or not; a shortfall at delivery is settled at the difference to the market; the price is never better than the expected price.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Futures traded with anyone but the trader.
  - **The spec sets:** Which crops, how much, the price's margin (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
