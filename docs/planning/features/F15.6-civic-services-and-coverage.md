### US-148 Civic buildings

As the player, I want civic buildings that work only when staffed and in reach, so that a school with no teacher is just a building.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S22
- **Acceptance:** From spec 023/026
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** The clinic (ADR 0107 §3: you build it and the doctor's house before the Township; the district pays the doctor, you then the treasury pay the allowance), and from the Township a watchman's post (crime down within reach), a community hall (belonging; festivals there) and a playground (leisure), built by the treasury. Each needs its staff (watchman, caretaker from the hiring board, wages in the budget) and has a reach and a capacity; homes out of reach get nothing. The school gets a reach too.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Police and fire stations, a hospital, a park, cinema or museum, a secondary school or library (the Town).
  - **The spec sets:** Costs, reaches, capacities (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

**Tasks** (spec 023 §3.1, §3.2, §3.6, §3.7, §3.9)

- [ ] T1: `ClinicState`, the clinic and quarters (shares, 48 h, the doctor's arrival), the effects and the benefit counts
- [ ] T2: the coverage rule (reach, capacity, staff) for the school and the clinic
- [ ] T3: the three civic buildings, their plots, the *Safety* part, the budget guard on the card
- [ ] T4: the appointments (`PostJob`, `DismissStaff`) and the doctor's leaving and return
- [ ] T5: the artifacts, then the sheets and cards by scripted touch in the Editor

### US-149 Festivals

As the player, I want to schedule festivals, so that my town celebrates together.

- **Points:** 3 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S24
- **Acceptance:** From spec 023/026
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** One festival a season, scheduled at the community hall or the square, paid by the treasury or as your gift: a happiness rise for its days, more customers at the shops, a Chronicle entry.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Tourists and visitors from outside (the Town).
  - **The spec sets:** Each festival's name, cost and size (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
