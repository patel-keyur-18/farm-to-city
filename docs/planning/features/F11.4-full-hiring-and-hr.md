## Notes for the spec (ADR 0039 §11)

- Make hiring feel rewarding through realistic levers, never by underpaying (the owner agreed, 2026-10-19): more land to put hands on, processing and markets that use the extra harvest, and skill growth that shows (a hand's hours rise each season). Keep wages at realistic rural levels (Phase 1: 30,000 a season for a resident hand).

### US-219 Your townspeople at work on your fields (owner request, 2026-10-28; ADR 0083)

As the player, I want the families living in my village houses to work my fields under my orders, so that the people I house work for my farm.

- **Points:** 3 (ADR 0083; was 5) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S15 (owner, 2026-10-29: same branch and PR #20)
- **Acceptance:** spec 015 AC-11; spec 014 AC-16

**What US-112 already gives (this sprint):** the hiring board lists your townspeople of working age as farm-hand candidates, their family house counting as housing (spec 015 §3.1); hired, a townsperson is a hand like any other, with standing orders. So a family member can be ordered onto your fields from Sprint 15's build.

**Pushback (2026-10-28):** work in return for housing alone, unpaid, is bonded labour (bandhua mazdoori), abolished in India by the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976; a game that teaches real lessons shouldn't reward it. The families also aren't fed by you: they buy their food from their own income (spec 014's budgets). The realistic version, and what this story would add:
