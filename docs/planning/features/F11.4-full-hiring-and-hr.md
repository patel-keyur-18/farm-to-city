### US-113 Productivity and training

As the player, I want workers' output to depend on skill, motivation and fatigue, so that managing people matters.

- **Points:** 8 (ADR 0076) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S15
- **Acceptance:** spec 015 AC-4–AC-7

**Tasks**

- [ ] US-113.T1 Output = skill × motivation × (1 − fatigue) for hands and business workers; motivation (fairness, home, events), fatigue, long days at 1.5×; neutral by default; golden replays regenerated once
- [ ] US-113.T2 Courses (+10 skill, 6,000, 2 days away, paid); renewals ask by skill
- [ ] US-113.T3 The four traits (creative, frugal, sociable, perfectionist) with their effects; no contradictory pairs
- [ ] US-113.T4 The worker's card: output in words and stars with its *Why?*; long days; Send on a course
- [ ] US-113.T5 Tests: `WorkerTests.Output_Neutral`, `Fatigue_Overtime`, `Course`, `Traits`

- **Points:** 5 (ADR 0076) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S15

**Tasks**

- [ ] US-114.T1 The five events (raise request, offer from elsewhere, sick leave, family emergency, bad batch) with chances, waiting cards and choices (`AnswerHrEvent`); alerts and notifications

- [ ] US-114.T3 Tests: `HrEventTests.Choices`, `Reputation`

## Notes for the spec (ADR 0039 §11)

- Make hiring feel rewarding through realistic levers, never by underpaying (the owner agreed, 2026-10-19): more land to put hands on, processing and markets that use the extra harvest, and skill growth that shows (a hand's hours rise each season). Keep wages at realistic rural levels (Phase 1: 30,000 a season for a resident hand).
