## Summary

Each household budgets its income by Engel's law, works in town or outside, buys its food at the local market, and is as happy as its needs are met; the People sheet's Town tab, the household and citizen cards and the HUD show it all with *Why?*.

### US-240 Rent: kutcha houses, location, yearly leases (ADR 0107 §8)

As the player who lets houses, I want rents that are fair for the kind of house, its place and its lease, so that being a landlord teaches what real rental income is.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S20
- **Source:** ADR 0107 §8 (amends ADR 0063 §2, ADR 0102 §3 (c); spec 014 amended in the same story)
- **Acceptance:**
  - The family house is the kutcha house (60,000), its base rent about 2,500 a season (`population.json`); rent = base × location × the price index, location from road access (district road 1.20, village lane 1.00, inner 0.85; `land.json` or `population.json`); the rent's *Why?* shows each part (`RentTests.KindLocationIndex`).
  - A lease runs 4 seasons at a fixed rent from move-in; at renewal it moves toward the market rent, the rise at most 10% (content) (`RentTests.YearlyLease`).
  - The renewal card offers the market rise (default), a smaller one or none; a smaller rise raises the tenant's happiness and loyalty; a rent past about 30% of the family's income may make it leave under the existing rules (`RentTests.RenewalChoice`, `RentViewTests.RenewalCard`). *As built (ADR 0110 §2–§4):* a raise never goes past what 30% of the income allows, so no raise pushes a family out; the choices are the market rise, half of it, keep, or (over 30%) lower by 5%, plus *Decide later*.
  - Houses already built keep cost and book value; a lease signed before keeps its rent until its renewal (`MigrationTests.Schema17_Leases`); a hand's house as part of his pay follows the kutcha rent from his next renewal (`RentTests.HandHouse`).
  - Save schema 18: each lease's start and rent; a `saves/schema18` fixture; every older schema loads.
  - Balance: the bots re-measure the Village's pace (`GateTests.Village`); a slip past 5 weeks with credit goes to the owner with its numbers; passive income's milestone day recorded (`FinanceGate.Measure`).
  - Editor: the renewal card, a rent's *Why?* and the house sheet by scripted touch; the card previewed to the owner before code.
