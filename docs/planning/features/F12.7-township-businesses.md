## Summary

The businesses Phase 3's systems need (ADR 0107 §7): a brick kiln for construction's bricks, a tuition centre that sells to richer households, a tempo that carries your goods on the new road, and a restaurant. The hardware store, private clinic, tailor, carpenter and a construction firm wait for Phase 4; the combine harvester is Won't.
