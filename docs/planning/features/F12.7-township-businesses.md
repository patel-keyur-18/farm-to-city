## Summary

The businesses Phase 3's systems need (ADR 0107 §7): a brick kiln for construction's bricks, a tuition centre that sells to richer households, a tempo that carries your goods on the new road, and a restaurant. The hardware store, private clinic, tailor, carpenter and a construction firm wait for Phase 4; the combine harvester is Won't.

### US-243 Brick kiln

As the player, I want to found a brick kiln, so that the town's building runs on my bricks, and a shortage of them really slows it.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S23
- **Acceptance:** From spec 025. Without a kiln, bricks come from the trader at the regional price, limited a day.
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A brick kiln on an acre you own (from the Village), worked by a hired kiln worker; fires outside the Monsoon; sells bricks to builders and the trader at its price; without a kiln, bricks come from the trader at the regional price, limited a day.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Cement and timber made in the town (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Output, inputs, the effect on the acre's soil (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-244 Tuition centre

As the player, I want a tuition centre, so that richer households spend on learning, and my town's skills grow.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S25
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**

### US-245 Tempo transport

As the player, I want a tempo to carry goods on the new road, so that my trips to the mandi cost less than a hired cart.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S25
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A tempo bought or leased (about 1,000 kg) carries your goods to the mandi instead of the hired trolley (cheaper a trip, on the paved road), and can be hired out to neighbours for trips as a business.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Trucks and a transport company (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Prices, capacity, trips a day (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
