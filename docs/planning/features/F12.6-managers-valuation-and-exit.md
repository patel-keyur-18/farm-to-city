### US-124 Managers

As the player, I want to hire a manager to run a business, so that I can scale beyond a few businesses.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S25
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** A manager role on the hiring board (skill; a style: cost-cutter, growth-chaser or quality-first; a wage) runs one business: its price preset, quality and stock by style, its worker hired; a seasonal report and a target you set (profit or growth); a managed business counts as passive income.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Managers of more than one business; a holding company (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each style's rules (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-125 Sell or expand

As the player, I want to sell a business or open branches, so that I learn valuation.

- **Points:** 5 (ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S25
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Each business shows its valuation (3–6× its annual profit, adjusted for growth and risk, explained); sell it to a buyer (a townsperson, a rival or an investor) over days, at a discount if rushed (card C); open a branch of the same kind on another acre or pitch.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Franchises (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The multiple's adjustments, the days and discount (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

### US-247 Investors, partners and buy-outs (ADR 0107 §7)

As the player, I want to take in an investor or a partner citizen, and to answer a rival's offer to buy my business, so that I learn what equity and a valuation mean.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S25
- **Source:** ADR 0107 §7 (spec 016's "investors and partner citizens in Phase 3"; ADR 0074 and 0095's buy-outs); spec 028 (to write)
- **Acceptance:** From spec 028
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** An investor puts in cash for a share of equity and profit; a partner citizen shares profit and decisions; a rival may offer to buy one of your businesses, accepted or refused (card C).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Shares traded on a market (the City).
  - **The spec sets:** The offers' terms (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).
