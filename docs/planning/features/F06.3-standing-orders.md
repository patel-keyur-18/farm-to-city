### US-186 The hand works every season

As the player, I want my hand to keep his fields sown, tended, harvested and sold in every season without me stepping in, so that a paid hand is worth his wage (owner, Phase 1 playtest, ADR 0048 §1–2).

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S10
- **Acceptance:** spec 006 AC-13, AC-24

**Tasks**

- [x] US-186.T1 `FarmRules.Estimate` (shared with the sow sheet) and `FarmRules.BestCrop`; `Orders.CropToSow`: the order's crop in season, else the hand's best pick; `OrderSpec.HandChooses` (crop −1); the offer minimum only for the order's own crop
- [x] US-186.T2 The order sheet's "Let Manoj choose" with today's best pick; an out-of-season crop says the hand picks in between; sowing by hand never rewrites the hand's choice
- [x] US-186.T3 The map: a hand under contract walks his own fields with work, then every other growing field; home only when nothing grows or unpaid
- [x] US-186.T4 Tests: `OrderTests.HandChoosesBestCrop` (AC-24), `KeepCrop_LiveAndAway` (AC-13), `PeopleViewTests` (the order sheet, `Map_Patrols_And_Homes`)
- [x] US-186.T5 Sprint 10 review (ADR 0049): every order acts at once (`orderActionsPerHour` 6), so orders on two fields given together are both worked; sowing by hand runs before an order change; crops drawn below people on the map
- [x] US-186.T6 ADR 0050: the hand's choice only among crops he can pay for; an unaffordable order crop falls back to his pick (`OrderTests.NeverIdleWhileACropIsAffordable`, AC-26; reproduced and verified in the Editor at 2,000 coins)
- [x] US-186.T7 Owner's device check (`s10-people-fixes.md`): passed on the iPhone 13, 2026-10-21
