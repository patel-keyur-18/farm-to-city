### US-140 Taxes

As the player, I want to set taxes, so that I fund public services.

- **Points:** 5 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S21
- **Acceptance:** Spec 022 AC-1 to AC-8, AC-12 to AC-16 (the tests it names)
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Property tax on every house in the town (yours and the old village's cohort included), a rate on land value (by house kind until land value exists, US-143); business tax on each business's season profit (yours included, from its own books); both collected in arrears at the close. You set each rate within bounds; before it's set, its revenue and its effect on households' budgets, happiness and migration are shown.
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Sales tax (it moves every shop's price; Phase 4 with the Town's markets); a tax ceiling set by approval (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** The bounds, and how happiness and migration answer a rate (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

**Tasks**

- [ ] T1: `treasury.json` `taxes`, the 15% guard in the loader; accounts 5270, 5830, 2400
- [ ] T2: house tax and business tax at the close through spec 021's intake; owed tax; the village's shops' reference profit
- [ ] T3: `SetTaxRate` and `UndoTaxRate` (from the next season); the preview in `Sim.View`
- [ ] T4: your side's rows (*Spent*, *Next season, already due*, the business's books, the house sheet)
- [ ] T5: the artifact preview, then the Taxes tab and the tour, in the Editor by scripted touch

### US-141 Policies

As the player, I want to enact policies with lags, so that I see winners and losers.

- **Points:** 8 (re-pointed, ADR 0107) · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S21
- **Acceptance:** Spec 022 AC-9 to AC-11, AC-13, AC-15, AC-16 (the tests it names)
- **Scope (ADR 0107 §13):**
  - **In Phase 3:** Five policies: free school meals, farm input subsidy, small business grant, minimum wage, rent control. Each shows its cost a season, its effect, its lag in seasons and its winners and losers before you enact it; at most 3 active; enacting and repealing on confirm cards. Subsidies and grants go to townspeople and neighbours, never to your own farm or businesses (ADR 0107 §6). Could: a night market (with the watchman, US-148).
  - **Not in Phase 3:** Tourism promotion (needs hotels, the Town); pollution limits (needs industry, Phase 4); policy slots set by council mood (Phase 4).
  - **The spec sets:** Each policy's cost, effect size and lag (numbers and wording only; the scope above is decided).

**Tasks**

- [ ] T1: the policies' content, states, slots, the re-enact wait, pausing; the seventh department
- [ ] T2: each effect (meals, subsidy and hangover, grant, minimum wage, rent control; night market Could)
- [ ] T3: `EnactPolicy` and `RepealPolicy` cards
- [ ] T4: the stress bots and `TaxGateTests.HandsOff`
- [ ] T5: the artifact preview, then the Policies tab, in the Editor by scripted touch
