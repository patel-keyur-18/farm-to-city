## Summary

Prove 2,000 walking agents at 60 fps on the iPhone 13 within half the frame budget, and prove the sim gives identical results on the iPhone (Phase 0 gate).

The spike's map, stub, build, measurement and pass rule are defined in ADR 0034 §8 (64 × 64 map, 2,000 agents, Release IL2CPP, 95th-percentile CPU and GPU ≤ 8.3 ms).

As the developer (Claude), I want a throwaway scene with 2,000 sprite agents on an isometric tilemap plus a sim tick stub, so that we know early whether the plan scales.

- **Points:** 5 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S3
- **Acceptance:** On the iPhone 13, sim plus rendering fit in 8.3 ms per frame
