### US-115 Found a business

As the player, I want to research, plan, fund and open a business, so that I become an entrepreneur.

- **Points:** 8 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S14
- **Acceptance:** spec 016 AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-9–AC-11, AC-14

**Tasks**

- [x] US-115.T1 `businesses.json` (types, the mill's rate, power, repairs, catchment, fee, loan and overdraft terms), goods atta and bran; loader and validation
- [x] US-115.T2 `Business`, `BusinessesState`, save schema 10 with migration and `saves/schema10`; `FoundBusiness` (era, owned acre, one an acre, funds, worker), building 24 h
- [x] US-115.T3 The mill's hour: the area's grain first (catchment + town), own wheat up to the daily amount at the market price, market wheat if allowed; yields; power; atta to the trader (400 kg a day) and market, bran to the feed room first; the worker (town hire, neighbour, or you with your farm hours)
- [x] US-115.T4 Screens: Businesses in the Menu, choose a type, research, the plan (with *Why?*), placement on lit acres, funding, worker; the business card (Today, Settings). Plan vs actual after 4 seasons moves to US-116 (the Books tab)
- [x] US-115.T5 The mill's art in Blender (shed, chakki, sacks, people with grain), layout previewed to the owner first; Editor tour with scripted touch
- [x] US-115.T6 Tests: `ProductionTests.Mill_Rate_Order_Yield`, `BusinessTests.InternalSale`, `RunItYourself`, `ChunkingInvariance`, `MigrationTests.Schema9_Businesses`, `BusinessViewTests.Plan_VsActual` (done with US-116); a business bot in the balancer (`MillBot`, `Gate.Mill`, `GateTests.Mill`, with US-117)

### US-117 Overdraft and failure

As the player, I want failing businesses to follow the overdraft path, so that failure teaches, not ends.

- **Points:** 3 · **Priority:** Must · **Sprint:** S14
- **Acceptance:** spec 016 AC-6, AC-7

**Tasks**

- [x] US-117.T1 The overdraft (half a season's costs, 4% a season); the business loan (60% of building and equipment, up to 12 seasons, your guarantee)
- [x] US-117.T2 Trouble: past the limit inputs stop, the unpaid worker leaves at the close; alerts and the card's what-to-do; `CloseBusiness` (stock to the trader, assets at 60%, debts repaid, the rest your loan); foreclosure takes a business on bought land only
- [x] US-117.T3 Tests: `BusinessTests.Overdraft_Trouble`, `Closing_Guarantee`, `BusinessLoan`, `BusinessViewTests.Loan_Trouble_Closing`, `GateTests.Mill`
