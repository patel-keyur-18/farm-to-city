As the developer (Claude), I want headless Blender rendering of Indian-specific assets, so that the valley looks like home.

- **Points:** 8 (4 after ADR 0039, + 4 for the ground and walk cycles, ADR 0041) · **Priority:** Should · **Sprint:** S6
- **Scope:** every remaining map sprite (field crops as whole-acre patches, garden beds, trees with their fruiting variant, the tree pit, the sale sign), the ground (grass, tilled soil, sand, water, the quarters, tile sides), the sheet and HUD icons, and 4-frame walk cycles for every person in two facings, rendered on the rig approved in Sprint 5; the seasonal tint stays in code (ADR 0041 §8)
