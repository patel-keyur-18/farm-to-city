## Summary

Six real Indian crops (methi, amaranth, okra, green peas, maize, mustard) give every season a crop on every rung of time: about 1 h, 2 h, 4 h, 6–8 h and 15 h. No farm formula changes. The sow sheet orders crops by growing time, says when each will be ready and what it earns an hour, and the balancer proves no new crop is a money loop or a season's new leader.
