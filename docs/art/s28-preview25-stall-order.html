Preview 25: Grow for the vegetable stall

Sprint 28, US-269 (spec 030 §3.6, §7.5). A hand can be told to grow for your vegetable stall. The stall's preorder is what it will need tomorrow: what it puts out, less its own stock, less what your store holds and what is already growing for it. The hand sows the vegetable with the biggest unmet preorder, keeps the harvest in your store, and the stall takes it at its 6:00 round. Nothing is sold to the trader.
