Preview 24: Supply, Automatic or Manual

Sprint 28, US-268 (spec 030 §3.4, §3.5, §7.4). A new Supply tab on a business's card shows what it took today, from you and bought, and lets you choose how much of your own produce it may take. Automatic is today's rule and stays the default. Manual caps each good a day. What you do not send stays in your store. Pick the situation in "Show" on each tab.
