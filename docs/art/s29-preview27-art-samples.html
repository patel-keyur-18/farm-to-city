Preview 27 · Three art samples, three looks

The same family house, wheat acre and farm hand with his wife, drawn three ways from the same models, the game's camera and the same light direction. Only the style differs. Pick the size first: the phone's opening view is how you see the farm most of the time.

Size:

Why these sizes? The game's opening view is 2.8 acres tall on the phone (OpeningHalfHeight 1.4), so on the iPhone 13 (1170 px tall, 460 ppi) an acre is 418 phone pixels, about 0.9 inches. On a MacBook screen (about 112 points an inch) 0.9 inches is 102 points: a sprite rendered at 528 px an acre is shown at 102 ÷ 528 = 0.193. The closest zoom fits 3 acres across the 2532 px screen, 844 phone pixels an acre, so 0.39. On another screen the true sizes are only roughly true.
