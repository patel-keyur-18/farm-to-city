Preview 26: Two towns

Sprint 29, US-270 and US-272 (spec 031 §7). Two towns on one phone, each with its own save, clock, away card and play log. Settings → Your towns lists them and switches; the open town's name leads the time card once two exist; notifications name their town and a tap opens it. Nothing here changes the sim: a town is a whole save. Buttons are live only as a picture; use the controls under the tabs to see each state.
