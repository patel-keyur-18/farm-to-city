# 0041. Saves hold everything hashed, old saves refused until the playtest, notifications from a simulated absence, regulars, and every sprite rendered now (Sprint 6 planning review)

- Status: Accepted; §6's "at least a day" superseded by ADR 0058 (12 hours)
- Date: 2026-10-19
- Deciders: Keyur Patel
- Amends: spec 007 §3.1, §3.4, §3.5, §4, §8 (AC-3, AC-10), §9; spec 006 §3.3, §8 (AC-4); spec 008 §3.13 (where Settings lives in Sprint 6); ADR 0036 (the first notification ask before the letter exists); ADR 0039 §1 (walk frames later)
- Related: ADR 0002 (away rules, the 72-hour cap), ADR 0023 (one town, no undo), ADR 0024 (the play log), ADR 0007 and 0036 (zero-budget art, the low-poly base), ADR 0040 (the yard and quarters)
- GDD sections: Offline play and saves (Save system, Local notifications), Citizens and workforce (Regulars), Art, audio and feel

## Context

The Sprint 6 planning review (2026-10-19) checked the save, notification and play-log stories (US-073 to US-077), regulars (US-067) and the full Blender render (US-171) against spec 007, spec 006, the code and the Sprint 5 additions (people, orders, the 2-acre yard, the labour quarters). Eight gaps were decided one by one.

## Decisions

1. **A save holds everything the state hash covers, plus the command log.** The seed, clock, scheduler and jobs, weather, land (the 2-acre yard and the quarters included), ledger, finance and reports, market and store, crops, garden, trees, credit, and people (the roster with seconds worked, applicants, hands and contracts, huts, the job post, orders with their kind, last action and the per-hand action counter); then the compacted command log and the replay base. Content references are written as **id strings** and resolved on load. One write path serves the hash and the save, so a field that is hashed can't be left out of a save; AC-1's round trip covers people and credit. **Not saved:** where people stand on the map (they restart at home or on their fields), the open sheet, pending events and the away card. The camera position is kept in the settings file.
2. **Old saves after a state change: refused and kept until the Phase 1 playtest build (Sprint 9), migrated from then on.** Until then a build that changes the state's fields raises `schemaVersion` and `minSchema` together; an older save is refused with a notice ("This town was saved by an older test build and can't be opened. It's kept on the phone; a new town has started."), and the file is kept as `town.unreadable-<date>.ftc` (exportable for a bug report), never deleted. From the playtest build on, every state change ships a migration with a test (spec 007 §3.1). Value tuning needs nothing: content is read at load. The header records the build and a content fingerprint, so a save's log is only replayed on matching content.
3. **Notifications come from a simulated absence, with no daily cap.** When the app goes to the background, the planner runs the away advance on a copy of the state up to the away cap (about 3 ms for 72 h on the iPhone 13) and notifies from the events that would happen, which are exact if the player stays away. This covers hands' orders, fields re-sown while away, and season-close outcomes, which known finish times miss. Importance (GDD): **urgent** (a loan payment missed or its final notice; a contract lapsed), **needs you** (a crop, bed or fruit ready that no order covers; the hand's hut built), **information** (hands' harvests and picks). There is **no daily cap** (owner); quiet hours stay (22:00–07:00, moved to 07:00 and grouped); items of a kind group within 30 minutes, and **information groups into at most one notification per 4 hours** (`infoGroupMinutes` 240), so a hand on a 2-hour radish order doesn't ping every 2 hours. iOS keeps at most 64 pending local notifications per app, so the plan keeps the first 64 by importance, then time. Text names the kind, the count and the crop ("2 fields of wheat are ready"), never a money amount. The 2-a-day rule of spec 007 and the GDD is withdrawn.
4. **Settings in Sprint 6: a gear button and a Settings sheet.** A round gear button under the Store chip opens a full-height Settings sheet, one row per choice with its own button: last saved and *Save now*, Notifications, Play log, *Export town*, *Import a town*, *Export play log*, *New game…* (a confirm card: *Export first*, *Replace*, *Cancel*). In Sprint 7 the Menu gets a Settings entry; US-084 shrinks from 2 points to 1 (number format, reduced motion, sound).
5. **Import from a list in Settings, no title screen.** *Import a town* lists every `.ftc` in the Documents folder with its file name, game date, cash and saved-at time and an *Import* button; a file that can't load shows why on its row ("from an older test build", "damaged"). Import uses the New game confirm card. No pop-up at launch (the game opens on the map, and its own exports are in the same folder). Exports are named `FarmToCity-<yyyy-MM-dd-HHmm>.ftc` until towns have names.
6. **The first notification ask is a plain card until the letter exists.** The first time a field crop of at least a day is sown, a card asks: "Your potato will be ready at 14:05 tomorrow. Want a nudge when it's ready? Never at night." *Yes* / *Not now*. iOS's permission dialog follows only a *Yes*. The second ask after the first season close is built now. Sprint 8 (US-089) moves the first ask into the letter's `comeBack` text; the rule of when to ask is unchanged.
7. **Regulars (US-067).** A regular joins the applicants even when 3 are waiting; always accepts an offer (they asked to come); has a skill drawn when they ask (seeded, the usual 20–70 range: known, not better) shown exactly, and two traits, the first their day-labour trait, both shown; if not hired within the 2-day wait goes back to day labour and never asks again; when hired leaves the roster and a new seeded neighbour takes their place, so the pool stays at 8. A regular asks only during live play (ADR 0002); days hired while away count.
8. **Every sprite and icon rendered in Blender this sprint, at full quality (owner: no more deferral).** On the rig approved in Sprint 5: field crops as whole-acre patches (5 crops × 3 growing stages and ready), garden beds (3 crops × 3 stages, the empty bed), trees (4 kinds × sapling, young, mature, in fruit), the tree pit and the sale sign, the ground (grass, tilled soil, sand, water, the quarters' beaten earth, tile sides), the icons (produce, inputs, weather, HUD and sheet icons), and **walk cycles** for every person (4 frames, two facings). The seasonal tint stays in code; fruiting trees have their own render. A style checkpoint comes first: a wheat acre, a mango tree, a ground patch and three HUD icons shown as Editor screenshots on the map; the batch follows the owner's approval. US-171 grows from 4 to 8 points.
9. **The notifications package:** `com.unity.mobile.notifications` 2.5.0, the version that ships with Unity 6000.3.26f1, pinned, used only by `IosNotificationService` behind `INotificationService` (spec 007 §3.5). If it fails on the iPhone 13, a small Objective-C plugin replaces it behind the same interface.

- **Planning:** Sprint 6 is 26 points (US-171 4 → 8); Sprint 7's US-084 is 1 point (Sprint 7: 15 points). F19.3's US-171 gets its tasks.
- **Players** may get more than 2 notifications on a busy day; the switch in Settings is always one tap away.
