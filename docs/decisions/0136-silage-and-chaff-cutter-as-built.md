# 0136. Silage and a chaff cutter as built: a pit that keeps the spare cut, a cutter that saves a fifth of the roughage, the third shed moved to Phase 4

- Status: Accepted (owner approved the three gaps, a correction to the second, and Preview 9, 2026-11-19)
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Deciders: Keyur Patel
- Amends: spec 025 §3.8 (US-255), ADR 0107 §15
- Related: ADR 0056, 0060, 0088, 0135; spec 012 §3.3, §3.7, §3.8b

## Context

Spec 025 §3.8 (ADR 0107 §15) gave the farm "silage that keeps green fodder over the dry season (fodder cost −15%), a chaff cutter that cuts the feed's hours 10%, a third shed". Its numbers were left to the story. Three things were decided with the owner one at a time (the second twice, after a payback table of mine was found to be per animal and not per herd), then shown in Preview 9.

## Decision

1. **The silage pit.** Built once, beside the shed (the first shed standing): `BuildSilagePit`, 10,000 × index, at once, capitalised to Buildings (1600), holds 20 t (`livestock.silage` in content). It is a number on the shed's books (`LivestockState.SilageGrams`), not a market good: adding a good would change every save's stock list. **The morning's spare cut** (what the fodder fields give beyond the herd's need) **goes into the pit instead of being sold standing at 1 a kg**, 85% of it (15% is lost making silage, real silage loses 10 to 20%); what the pit has no room for is sold standing as before. **The pit feeds before anything is bought**: after the commons and the fields' cut, the herd eats silage, then green fodder bought from neighbours. A kg put by gains about 1.57 over selling it (0.85 × 3.05 − 1.02, at index 1.017), so a full 20 t pit gains about 31,000 on 10,000. It pays only if the fields grow more than the herd eats; the sheet says so. The spec's "Monsoon only" rule was not kept: any spare, any season, is one line to learn.
2. **The chaff cutter.** Bought once, 30,000 × index, at once, capitalised to Equipment (1500), the first shed standing, no upkeep. **The herd's green fodder and straw need falls by 20%** (`livestock.chaffCutter`; the ground cattle feed is not chopped); a heifer's share and the silage's too. 20% is the real range (chaffing saves a fifth to a third of the roughage), and 30,000 the real price of a motor-driven cutter (30,000 to 45,000): a herd of 4 pays it back in about 4 seasons, 8 in under 2, one animal in about 14 (the sheet's worked figure, `LivestockRules.ChaffSavingPerDay`, counts only feed that would be bought; fodder and straw from your own fields cost nothing in the books, so there it saves little, and the *Why?* says so). The first proposal (20,000, 20%) paid a herd of 4 back in 2.4 seasons, quicker than real, and the table I showed for it was per animal; corrected before any code.
3. **The third shed is cut** to Phase 4's dairy farms (ADR 0107 §13): the two-shed assumption sits in nine files (the shed rules, the map and its drawing, the field sheets, the save's 0 or 1, the bullocks' home, equipment, the tests), and four more places would not be what limits a player who has 8 animals on 6 lakh of cattle. The sheet says it in words: a *Sheds* row, "1 of 2" or "2 of 2", with "Two sheds is the most a farm yard keeps. A dairy farm business in the Town & City phase takes more." and a *Why?*.
4. **Where it is.** A *Feed stores* block on the shed sheet, after the feed rows: the silage pit (not built / empty / stock and days it lasts if the fields gave nothing), the chaff cutter (not bought / working), each with its honest note, a green button (tap confirm card with the price) and a *Why?* (`silagePit`, `chaffCutter`, `shedLimit`). The green fodder row says "silage" and "N spare went to the pit (M lost)". The away card gets *Silage put by* and *Silage fed instead of bought fodder*. The pit is drawn on the cattle acre in front of the animals (a brick-lined pit under a dark tarpaulin held down with tyres), and the two new pictures are Blender renders (`silage_pit`, `chaff_cutter`).

## Consequences

- Save schema 21 gains `LivestockState.SilagePit`, `SilageGrams`, `ChaffCutter` and the feeding report's three silage numbers (late: hashed once used); the two schema 21 fixtures were regenerated; older saves read unchanged. `BuildSilagePit` and `BuyChaffCutter` are new command tags; `SilagePitBuilt`, `ChaffCutterBought` new events, and `AnimalsFed` gains `SilageFed` and `SilagePut`.
- The pit and the cutter are kept at cost, like animals (no depreciation in Phase 3); neither is sellable or collateral.
- Tests: `SilageTests` (9: the two purchases and their refusals, the cutter's fifth, the spare going in at 85%, no pit selling it as before, the pit feeding before buying, a full pit, the worked saving, the save), `SilageViewTests` (4), the confirm, view-keys and words coverage. Editor tour by scripted touch: `docs/art/s23-255-*.png`; render statistics: the pit is one sprite; the flags are a set-pass call of 8 to 9 that earlier readings also had, no drawing growth over 10%.
- Balance to read at the sprint-end pass: how many herds buy the cutter and when, how often the pit ever fills, and whether the cutter at 30,000 is bought before the second shed.
