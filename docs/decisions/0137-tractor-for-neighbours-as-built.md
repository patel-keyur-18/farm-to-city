# 0137. Your tractor for neighbours as built: eight acres a morning at 80% of the day's hire, diesel and wear yours, in farm income and never in passive income

- Status: Accepted (owner approved the two gaps and Preview 10, 2026-11-19)
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Deciders: Keyur Patel
- Amends: spec 025 §3.8 (US-256), spec 013 §2 ("hands driving the tractor for neighbours: Phase 3"), ADR 0107 §15
- Related: ADR 0075, 0088, 0089, 0125 (G7), 0136; spec 013 §3.2, spec 025 §3.8

## Context

Spec 025 §3.8 (ADR 0107 §15, ADR 0125 G7) gave the farm "your tractor for neighbours: hire it out by the day when idle, 80% of the hire rate, passive income, never the best use of it", with a demand cap and an exploit check (US-248) so that buying a tractor on a loan to hire it out is no loop. The numbers and where the money shows were left to the story. Two things were decided with the owner one at a time, then shown in Preview 10.

## Decision

1. **A daily rule, all numbers in content.** At the 6:00 pass (`EquipmentRules.Daily`), if the switch is on, the machine is **bought** (a lease is rented for your own fields: `LeasedMachine`), working (not broken, its diesel paid) and a **hired hand** who is not on sick leave or a course can drive it (`HasDriver`; `NoHandToDrive`), it works **`customAcresPerDay` = 8** acres for neighbours: 8 is about a day's ploughing for a 35 HP tractor, and neighbours' farms are small, so the cap is flat and **does not grow with your town**. Only the tractor can (the plough has 0). The fee is `customFeeShare` = **80%** of the day's hire for those acres (2,000 for the 20 fields a hired day covers is 100 an acre, so 80 an acre, 640 a day at index 1); the **diesel is yours** (600 an acre a season over 20 fields, 30 an acre, 240 a day); and **wear**: on a day it works for neighbours its daily chance of breaking is multiplied by `customWear` = 2 (0.5% to 1% kept up, 4% to 8% with maintenance skipped), a repair costing 5% of what the machine cost. The hand's wage is already paid, so the hand costs nothing more. One cash entry a morning: Dr Cash (fee − diesel), Dr Power (diesel), Cr 4040 (fee).
2. **Farm income, not passive income** (the owner approved this against the spec's wording). Account **4040 Machine hire received** is in *Farm income*, because the diesel and repairs it costs are farm costs: in *Other income* the season would show the costs without the earnings. It is not counted in the hub's *passive income covers living* (`Hub.Passive`): your hand drives it, which is work, and counting it would flatter that figure. The season page lists it as *Machine hire received* with a tractor icon; the diesel is in *Power*.
3. **The exploit check (US-248 reads it).** `HireNetPerSeason` is the fee less diesel less the expected extra repairs, for the days of a season at the cap: **about 1,000 a season** with the tractor kept up. The machine's own cost of standing there is its depreciation (price ÷ 60, 10,000) plus its maintenance (1.5%, 9,000): about 19,000 a season. Hiring out helps with running costs and **never pays for the tractor**, so buying one on a loan to hire it out loses money every season. Fee and diesel both follow the price index, so the ratio holds at any index (`HireOutTests.TheCap_Holds_AndHiringOutNeverPaysForTheTractor` runs it at ×3). **Found while building:** with maintenance **skipped** (4% a day, 8% on hire days) the expected repairs are more than the fee leaves, so the sheet says *Loses N a season*: skipping upkeep and hiring out is the worst of both, and the sheet shows it.
4. **Where it is.** Menu → Equipment, on the tractor, a *Hiring out* block after its book and maintenance rows (not on a leased tractor, whose row says so): *Hire out to neighbours* (Off, or *On · 8 acres a day*, *resting* when broken, *standing idle* when its diesel is unpaid, *no hand to drive it*) with a **Switch on / Switch off** button (off by default, no confirm card: it costs nothing), *This season so far* (acres, earned, diesel; from the season's entries), and *Does it pay?* in words with the sum. Three *Why?* pages (`hireFee`, `hireCost`, `hirePays`). The away card gets one row, *Your tractor: Ploughed N acres for neighbours: X* with the diesel (`MachineHiredOut` events, `AwayRow.Tractor`). **Not drawn**: neighbours' acres are not your fields, so the map is unchanged.

## Consequences

- Save schema 21: `Machine.HireOut` (written from schema 21, hashed once on), account 4040 (late: `SinceSchema` 21, hashed once used); the schema 21 fixtures were regenerated; older saves read unchanged. `SetHireOut` is a new command tag (appended), `MachineHiredOut` a new event, `LeasedMachine` and `NoHandToDrive` new refusals.
- Content: `farming.json` machines `customAcresPerDay` (ox plough 0, tractor 8) and equipment `customFeeShare`, `customWear`; the loader checks the acres never exceed what the machine covers.
- Tests: `HireOutTests` (8: the switch's refusals, a morning's fee and diesel, no work when off, broken, unpaid or with no hand, the wear's doubled chance against the seeded draw, the cap and the exploit check at ×3 the index, farm income and not passive, the save, chunking), `HireOutViewTests` (7), the confirm, view-keys and words coverage. Editor tour by scripted touch: `docs/art/s23-256-*.png`; render statistics unchanged (the story draws only UI: 1061, 467 and 340 draw calls in the three views, no metric over 10%); the Editor saves restored identical.
- Balance to read at the sprint-end pass: how many players switch it on, and whether a tractor with upkeep paid and a hand never idle is worth the 1,000 a season (it is meant to be a small thing; the lesson is custom hiring covers running costs, not the machine).
