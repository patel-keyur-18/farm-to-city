# 0167. Sprint 26 as built: the Chronicle, the Almanac, citizen stories, your kirana with marketing and khata, supervisors with accidents, quarrels and associations

- Status: Accepted (the owner approved each story and each preview, 2026-11-24)
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Deciders: Keyur Patel
- Related ADRs: 0027, 0095, 0153, 0160, 0164, 0165, 0166

## Context

Sprint 26 built six stories on save schema 24 (shipped once, with fixtures `schema24.ftc` and `schema24-people.ftc`): US-157 the Chronicle, US-158 the Almanac, US-159 citizen stories (spec 029), US-260 your kirana, marketing and khata, US-259 supervisors, accidents, quarrels and associations (spec 028 §3.9 and §3.10), and US-248, the exploit checks (ADR 0168). Gaps 3 to 13 of ADR 0166 were closed one per message before their code, and Previews 19, 20 and 21 were shown as artifacts before any screen. This ADR records what was decided and how it was built; the numbers of the balance pass are in ADR 0168.

## Decisions

1. **The Chronicle (US-157).** A town's history is a list of at most 400 compact entries (time, kind, two numbers and a content id); the sentence is made when the sheet opens, so wording and language can change. 22 kinds, 14 of them once-only and pinned with the eras and the founding. Entries are written from events by one `ChronicleRules` pass in a fixed order, away events with their own times. A record harvest needs 1,000 kg and 5% over the best earlier season of the crop. Gap 10: events that lacked a field (the epidemic's sick, a loan's lender) were given it. Hashed only once it holds an entry; an older save begins its Chronicle at the load with the eras it has reached.
2. **The Almanac (US-158).** One card at each Winter close after *Your year*: households, people, net worth, the best return, the costliest loss and a person to remember; the last 8 are kept. Gap 12: the costliest loss is a running worst kept at the moment it happens (a fire's repair as paid, a loss on a sale or closing, a day's spoiled goods, a seizure or default, a business's losing season), with no ledger scan. G10: a crop's money put in is its seed, fertiliser and its share of the year's hired labour by sowings. Net worth is in the coins of the first year, so rising prices are not growth.
3. **Citizen stories (US-159).** Four arcs over real events: a hand's stall, a family's first reader, a long life, a migrant family that stays; at most one step a season across the town, a toast and a Story line for each, and an away line. Gap 11: the story founds the hand's stall itself at the leaving close (a newcomer of his trade on a free pitch or the lane's verge, owned by him), so a hand is never left for nothing. A finished arc is remembered (`Retired`) so a story never happens twice. Amended by the balance pass (ADR 0168): the stall story starts at the Township and waits for your shop's first profitable season.
4. **Your kirana, marketing and khata (US-260).** A new shop kind *grocery* (gap 7): the staples' shares of a basket (atta 35, rice 25, lentils 10, ghee 5, rusk 5, pav 10, paneer 5, curd 5) against the village grocer's staples, which compete only once a kirana of yours is open. Gap 6: a regular is a household of yours that the open shop of the kind attracts most; the old village's families are never on khata. **Marketing** is paid at once and again at each close (a rise pays the difference; a tap card asks); **khata** lets a regular take its people's share of what the shop sold the town on credit up to the limit, losses by income (8, 4, 1% never paid, 5% a season late), and a household you cut off stays cut off. Gap 9: a new expense account *Bad debt*. A shop that closes or is sold collects what is owed it first (`KhataRules.Settle`). The kirana's kilos are 0.13 a person a day (gap 8: measured, not the spec's estimate), its goods bought from a wholesaler at the trader's price.
5. **Supervisors, accidents, quarrels and associations (US-259).** The supervisor is a saved record, not a Worker: a sixth post on the hiring board for the farm with 3 or more hands, migrants only, who covers the first 5 hands by id with +1.2% output a star (stars 1 + skill ÷ 20), halves accident chances and settles quarrels for you. Accidents roll daily for each worker at work; quarrels need 2 workers; an association needs 4 and forms on low pay, tiredness or a poor name. Gap 4: all three are cards with a 24 hour deadline and a free default (pay the hours; let it be; refuse), answered live or away by the hourly pass and told on the away card; they do not count toward spec 027's two waiting cards. An accident's bill is paid at the accident and saved with the card. A talk or a negotiation says how it went. Associations are the place's, not one worker's, so letting any hand go leaves the card.
6. **Schema 24 as one section each.** The Chronicle, Almanac and stories sections, the business growth block (marketing, khata), the newcomer's `Owner` and the workplace section are each hashed only when used and read by schema, so every older save loads with them empty; the goldens did not move.

## Consequences

- A town that answers nothing still works: cards take their defaults, khata and marketing stay as set, the treasury is never below zero (`GrowthLedgerTests.LeftAlone`).
- Every number on the new sheets has a *Why?* in words, with its content figures read when the sheet opens.
- The supervisor's price is tuned in ADR 0168.
